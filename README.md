Commercial Resilience Simulator

The Commercial Resilience Simulator is an AI-powered training application designed to help procurement and supply-chain professionals practise responding to realistic commercial disruptions in a safe, simulated environment.

Building on the interactive approach used in Off Book, the simulator allows participants to work through evolving supply-chain crises, make decisions under pressure, and see how their choices influence subsequent events. Each exercise is designed to test decision-making, risk assessment, investigation and communication.

The initial proof of concept focuses on a fictional supply chain, with interactive crisis scenarios spanning four or five rounds. The simulation maintains a record of previous decisions so that new developments can reflect the team's actions, leading to a structured after-action review highlighting strengths, risks and opportunities for improvement.

The longer-term vision is to explore how AI-powered simulations could help organisations identify weaknesses in their supply chains and improve their resilience before real-world disruptions occur.

Key objectives

Simulate realistic supply-chain disruptions using AI.

Support multi-round scenarios with evolving events and consequences.

Maintain a persistent record of decisions and scenario developments.

Encourage critical thinking, risk assessment and informed decision-making.

Generate structured feedback and after-action reviews.

Development status: Proof of concept.
